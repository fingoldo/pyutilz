# 08 Test suite quality audit (read-only)

Scope: `tests/` (362 Python files) checked for the test-quality class: asserts that check nothing, tests that patch the unit under test, order or global-state coupling, machine-dependent timing or CPU checks, skips and xfails without a valid reason, and duplicated bodies. Method: AST scan of every `test_*` function for a check-free body and for identical bodies, grep of skip/xfail/timing/`sys.modules`/reload sites, reading of the flagged sites, and running the shared `pyutilz.dev.code_audit` scanners on copies of the flagged files. No test was run and no file was edited.

Existing gates checked: `tests/test_meta/test_no_value_bearing_asserts.py` (scans `src/pyutilz` only, so it cannot see any test finding), `tests/test_meta/test_no_module_reload.py` (reload, `sys.modules` pop and subscript write, with a reviewed whitelist), and `tests/test_meta/test_code_audit_tests_baseline.py` (runs the test-quality scanners over `tests/`, its baseline is empty, and `duplicate_function_body` is excluded from its check list).

Checked and not findings: no `xfail` marker exists in `tests/` outside scanner fixture strings (`tests/code_audit/test_nondiscriminating_test.py`), so no tracking-reason gap exists. All 81 `pytest.skip`/`skipif` sites outside `code_audit/` carry a reason string. The 9 `assert True`/check-free functions the AST scan listed are either delegations to shared `assert_*` helpers in `py_ci_shared` or meta-gates, or fixtures held in a string literal (`tests/test_architecture_audit_20260903.py:176-192` is a scanner fixture inside `_TESTS_DIR_FIXTURE`, not a real test). `tests/test_polarslib_extra2.py` is the only duplicate-body pair found among bodies of three or more statements. `sys.modules` writes and reloads outside the whitelist were not found; the gates catch that class.

Verdict. The suite has no P0 and no P1 finding. The defects cluster in two places: wall-clock bounds and ordering assertions that depend on the host (T-01, T-02, T-04, T-05, T-06), and weak or duplicated assertions in a few test modules (T-03, T-10). The existing test-quality gate covers the textual wall-clock and vacuous-assert patterns, but its scanner misses bounds on a collected list (`min(elapsed)`) and timing-derived winners, and the reload gate misses `del sys.modules`, while its whitelist reason for `test_llm_config.py` (T-08) is contradicted by the source. The highest-value fixes are T-08 (correct the gate's reason), T-03 (add real assertions and remove the duplicate), and replacing the three host-timed ranking tests with an injected timer (T-04 and its siblings).

Counts: P0 0, P1 0, P2 4 (T-01, T-03, T-04, T-08), P3 7 (T-02, T-05, T-06, T-07, T-09, T-10, T-11).
